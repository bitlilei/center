Featured Project代表项目

Multimodal Behavioral Intelligence: From Motion to Meaning多模态行为智能：从动作到意义

This project connects video, 3D motion, language, and interaction to study human behavior. The video presents a research thread spanning representation learning, multi-person interaction, intention analysis, long-video evaluation, and robust motion recovery.该项目连接视频、三维运动、语言与交互信息，研究复杂人类行为的理解机制。视频集中展示从表征学习、多人交互、意图分析，到长视频评测与复杂环境运动恢复的研究链条。

Professor Huaping Zhang 张华平教授

Professor, School of Computer Science and Technology, Beijing Institute of Technology 北京理工大学计算机学院教授

Professor Huaping Zhang is the Director of the NLPIR Laboratory and Secretary-General of the Multilingual Intelligent Information Processing Technical Committee of the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence. His research interests include multilingual intelligent information processing, big data search and mining, natural language processing, and intelligent information analysis. 张华平教授现任NLPIR实验室主任、中国人工智能学会多语种智能信息处理专委会秘书长，主要研究方向包括多语种智能信息处理、大数据搜索与挖掘、自然语言处理以及智能信息分析。
